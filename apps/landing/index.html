Noah's Ark is an amphibious delivery drone with an AI brain on AWS. It flies in rain, lands on water, and finishes or safely aborts a mission with no signal.

Built for GDEX's hardest last mile: interior Sabah and Sarawak, flood zones, and the places roads don't reach.

JUNGLE
LAST-MILE

GDEX parcels to interior communities in Sabah and Sarawak: 20 km out with 2 kg, drop, 20 km home, 20% in reserve.

FLOOD
RELIEF

Food and medicine to people cut off by floods. Pontoons inflate in the air, so the Ark lands on water and waits.

DEFENCE
RESUPPLY

Ark-1M, an unarmed forward-resupply variant: 4 kg payload, low-signature skin, self-sealing pontoons.

How it works

FROM A CALL FOR HELP TO A DROP ON THE DOORSTEP.

The cloud plans, dispatches and forecasts. The drone flies, sees and decides. Each step is an event, so nothing waits on a link that might drop.

  1. 01 · SOS

    ASK

    A call for help from the Ark SOS app, SMS or WhatsApp, with the caller's location attached.

  2. 02 · TRIAGE

    UNDERSTAND

    Amazon Bedrock reads the message in any of four languages, scores urgency and lists what's needed.

  3. 03 · PLAN

    ROUTE

    Bedrock and Amazon Location Service pick the route, the drone and the payload, around geofences and weather.

  4. 04 · DISPATCH

    SEND

    Step Functions runs the flow and AWS IoT Core sends the mission to the drone over MQTT.

  5. 05 · DELIVER

    FLY

    ARK-001 flies it, with edge AI on an NVIDIA Jetson under IoT Greengrass, and lands on land or water.

OFFLOAD MANAGER: each task runs on the drone or in the cloud, chosen by link quality, battery and urgency. Lose the link, and the Ark still finishes or aborts safely.
