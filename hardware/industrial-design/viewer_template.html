What changed from the base model

  • Scaled so the motors sit on Ark's arm radius; the props come out at 28.6 in, the real G28.
  • Gimbal removed with its damping mount and board.
  • Inflatable pontoons on the skids, CO2-inflated for a water landing.
  • Cargo connector under the hub, with a 2 kg drop pod.
  • Battery moved inside, into the hub's belly well, with the avionics deck, ESCs and harness built around it; motor pods widened to the MN801-S.
  • Radar altimeter and GNSS mast added.
