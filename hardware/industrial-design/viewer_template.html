What changed from the base model

  • Scaled so the motors sit on Ark's arm radius; the props come out at 28.6 in, the real G28.
  • Gimbal removed; Ark carries cargo on a latch with smart contacts under the hub.
  • Battery inside the hub's belly well, sized for the mission on this airframe's own drag; the old battery bay is a sealed service hatch.
  • Electronics built 1:1 from a netlist: every part, port and wire, checked for current, voltage and fit.
  • Inflatable pontoons fired by the flight controller from the radar height during a water-landing descent.
  • Motor pods widened to the MN801-S; radar altimeter, GNSS mast and livery added.
